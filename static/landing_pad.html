🎯 Solo Landing Pad

The pad the Cockpit's pad finder looks for. Print it (or paint it) at any size, put it flat on the ground, and enter the black square's side in the Cockpit under 🎯 PRECISION → ⚙ PAD.

Painting it by hand

  • The outer ring of cells is the white margin: keep it, the pad is not found without it.
  • Matte paper or flat paint: a shiny pad reflects the sky and looks white.
  • Keep it flat and weighted down; prop wash lifts a loose sheet.
  • A 24 in (61 cm) pad is found from about 10-15 m up with the GoPro on Wide (less on a slow phone). Bigger pads are found from higher.
  • Printed mirrored or with a wrong cell, the pad is not found at all, so nothing wrong is ever sent to the Solo.
